**TomoriBot** is a customizable Discord AI chatbot. Give her an API key, and she chats
naturally in your server — remembering people, switching between personas, searching the
web, generating images and voice, and running tools on your behalf. You can invite the
public instance in seconds, or self-host your own for full control over your data and models.

## What TomoriBot Can Do

Every card below links to a full guide. New here? The [Quickstart](/introduction/quickstart/)
walks you from inviting the bot to your first conversation.

<div class="feature-grid">
  <FeatureCard title="Multiple Personas" img="/img/docs/intro/01-multiple-personas.png" href="/features/multiple-personas/">
    Run several characters in one server, each with its own personality, trigger words,
    and webhook avatar — and let them talk to each other.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Memory" img="/img/docs/intro/02-memory.png" href="/features/memory/">
    Teach her facts about people and your server. She remembers across conversations,
    and personal memories follow you between servers.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Media Generation" img="/img/docs/intro/03-media-generation.png" href="/features/media-generation/">
    Generate images, short videos, and spoken voice messages right in chat — from a
    prompt or by editing a reference image.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Agentic Tools" img="/img/docs/intro/04-agentic-tools.png" href="/features/tools-and-extensions/">
    Web search, URL reading, scheduled reminders, cross-channel messages, and custom
    MCP tools — she decides when to use them.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Fully Local Setup" img="/img/docs/intro/05-fully-local-setup.png" href="/self-hosting/local-endpoints/">
    Point her at your own hardware: local LLMs, ComfyUI, SearXNG, and local voice
    servers. Your hardware, your data.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Server Moderation" img="/img/docs/intro/06-server-moderation.png" href="/features/server-moderation/">
    Admin controls for cost and safety: quotas, channel and role whitelists, thought
    logs, and a bring-your-own-key requirement.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Command Reference" img="/img/docs/intro/07-command-reference.png" href="/features/command-reference/">
    Dozens of slash commands for deep customization — every one of them documented in a
    single searchable reference.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="SillyTavern Support" img="/img/docs/intro/08-sillytavern-support.png" href="/features/sillytavern-support/">
    Import SillyTavern prompt presets and character cards to bring existing characters
    straight into Discord.
  </FeatureCard>
</div>
