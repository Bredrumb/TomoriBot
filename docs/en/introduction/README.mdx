TomoriBot is a free and open-source AI agent and roleplaying system for Discord. The project aims to bring personal AI companions and role-play characters right into Discord servers, inspired by SillyTavern and Discord's discontinued Clyde. Simply put, she (or *it*) is an agentic + roleplaying harness for AIs put into Discord:

![Tomori Formula](/img/docs/intro/00-tomori-formula.png)
<small style={{ display: 'block', textAlign: 'center', fontStyle: 'italic', color: 'var(--sl-color-gray-3)' }}>Like an unholy trinity of Discord, SillyTavern, and OpenClaw</small>

Read as Tomo(友)-Ri(里), roughly meaning "Home of Friends", Tomori is a home for your very own practical assistant(s), customizable companion(s), and/or role-play partner(s) for yourself, or for everyone in your Discord server.

You can invite the public instance now or self-host your own for full control over your data (more info at [Quickstart](/introduction/quickstart/)):

## What can TomoriBot do?


<div class="feature-grid">
  <FeatureCard title="Multiple Personas" img="/img/docs/intro/01-multiple-personas.png" href="/features/chatting-personality/multiple-personas/">
    Run several custom characters in one server, each with their own settings, memories, and avatars. Watch them coordinate (or mess around) with each other!
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Natural Nudges" img="/img/docs/intro/09-natural-nudges.png" href="/features/chatting-personality/natural-nudges/">
    Personas can start conversations and join in on their own w/o mentions and triggers, making them feel more human.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Persistent Memory" img="/img/docs/intro/02-memory.png" href="/features/knowledge/memory/">
    Make your Tomori grow by teaching her facts about people and your server. She remembers across conversations, and personal memories follow you between servers.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Agentic Tools" img="/img/docs/intro/04-agentic-tools.png" href="/features/capabilities/tools-and-extensions/built-in-tools/">
    TomoriBot can autonomously web search, schedule reminders, block users, utilize custom
    MCP tools, and more.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Media Generation" img="/img/docs/intro/03-media-generation.png" href="/features/capabilities/media-generation/image-generation/">
    Generate/edit images, short videos, and spoken voice messages right in chat from a
    prompt or through reference.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Fully Local Setup" img="/img/docs/intro/05-fully-local-setup.png" href="/self-hosting/local-endpoints/">
    Create your own privately owned setup with local LLMs, ComfyUI, SearXNG, local voice
    servers, and MCPs.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="SillyTavern Support" img="/img/docs/intro/08-sillytavern-support.png" href="/features/integrations/sillytavern-support/">
    Import SillyTavern prompt presets and character cards to bring existing instructions and characters straight into Discord.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="Server Moderation" img="/img/docs/intro/06-server-moderation.png" href="/features/setup-administration/server-moderation/">
    Add admin controls for cost and safety: quotas, channel and role whitelists, thought
    logs, and BYOK requirements.
  </FeatureCard>
  <FeatureCard title="In-Discord Management" img="/img/docs/intro/07-command-reference.png" href="/features/command-reference/">
    Customize personas, models, tools, and more with native Discord slash commands.
  </FeatureCard>
</div>
