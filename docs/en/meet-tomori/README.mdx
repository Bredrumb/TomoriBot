What's an AI harness project without its cute mascot and fictional story? Lorewise, a **Tomori** is a android type, characterized by their mechanical horns, tail, and stand-out barcode tags (usually attached on their ear). They're capable of shapeshifting (the reason she can split into multiple personas).

There are multiple different production lines for Tomoris, most of which are for assisting humans but as seen below, each have different specialties, built-in personalities, and stories in the *Tomoriverse*:
